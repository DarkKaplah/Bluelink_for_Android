# Bluelink_for_Android
This is a port of Andy Fuse's IOS "Bluelink" app

At the time I was unaware of any other Android port of Andy's code.  As I wanted to tinker and relearn android development I decided to pick this up.  I will fully admit I took advantage of AI coding using the free Google Gemini and locally hosting Gemma 4.  This is mostly a straight port with a few adjustments for android.  Once you log in your battery SOC and range will display.  The HVAC toggle works. Email from the car confirms it's starting.  Closing and reopening the app corrects this for what testing I've done and you can stop the HVAC.  I'm a Kia EV6 owner in the US so YMMV.  Feel free to download this and modify it as you need.

I made this port to fit my needs.  The key remote is unreliable and the range is pitiful.  This way I can be sure my car has started and is defrosted for school drop off.

Anyway I hope this is of use to others.  I'm not claiming to be the only port of this.  Just found another similar one today.  Andy's the one who deserves kudos.  I'm posting this under the same license as that is what's right.
